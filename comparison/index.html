When ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews are asked the five queries a Wisconsin butter burger fan, a Packers tailgater, or a Green Bay tourist types tonight from a hotel near Lambeau, Kroll's surfaces in 1 of 5. The other four go to Solly's Grille, Al's Hamburger Shop, AJ Bombers, and Culver's. The 89 years and the charcoal grill are real. The structured data telling AI search the brand exists is not.
